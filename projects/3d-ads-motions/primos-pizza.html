3D Advertising Concept

PRIMO'S PIZZA

Category: Dual-Sided 3D Music Concept

A creative 3D advertising concept based on the idea of a violin playing music.

I connected the violin's shape with the brand's pizza concept, combining a pizza visual on one side and the Primo's Pizza logo on the other. Since the installation is designed in 3D, it can be viewed from both sides, making the concept visually interesting from different angles.

The project includes different views of the 3D design.

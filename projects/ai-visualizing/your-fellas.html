Your Fellas

Characters

Storyboarding Scenes

Visual Treatment

Project Story

Your Fellas is a production company that was just starting out. The advertisement introduces the company through a mysterious and imaginative campaign across downtown Cairo.

The story begins on an ordinary street in downtown Cairo, where several billboards are displayed.

Suddenly, the billboards begin showing the word Your Fellas. People notice the unexpected message, become curious, and start looking around and up at the sky.

A hot-air balloon then descends from the sky, carrying a large banner displaying the Your Fellas name.

As people continue watching in amazement, a flying saucer appears. A mysterious character with Lucifer-inspired wings emerges, accompanied by other characters.

The mysterious character asks people what they wish they could create.

Dream Sequence One: The Compound on the Moon

A girl says she dreams of creating a campaign for her residential compound, but she wants it to be set on the moon.

The character takes her upward, transporting her to the surface of the moon, where she discovers residential units presented in an imaginative lunar setting.

The sequence then transitions back by focusing on the girl's face and returning to the original street scene.

Dream Sequence Two: The Fashion Campaign

Another girl says she wishes to create a modeling session for her dress collection inside an ancient Egyptian temple. She is a fashion designer.

The advertisement then transitions into her imagined fashion shoot inside the temple.

After the scene, the story returns to the main encounter.

Ending

The final sequence closes with a focus on the mysterious character's face, followed by the Your Fellas brand name.

The central message is that Your Fellas can turn your production dreams into reality, no matter how imaginative or ambitious they are.

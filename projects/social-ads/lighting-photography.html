Lighting Photography

Creative Experiment: Light Painting Photography & Advertising

This project is an unofficial creative experiment exploring the use of neon colors in advertising through a connection with classical paintings.

The creative concept was inspired by famous masterworks, including Saturn Devouring His Son by Francisco Goya, The Scream by Edvard Munch, and The Starry Night by Vincent van Gogh. I connected the visual language and colors of these paintings with neon lighting to create a series of visually striking advertising posters.

The project was executed using Light Painting Photography, a photography technique that captures the movement of light through long exposure.

Original Photography & Hands-On Execution: I personally photographed and created these visuals myself. This is authentic original photography created with long-exposure techniques — not AI-generated artwork, not stock photography, and not a collection of downloaded images.

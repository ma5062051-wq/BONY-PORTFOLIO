EZAY MARKETING AGENCY

Industry: Marketing & Advertising Agency

A marketing agency project with a distinctive visual direction.

Instead of following conventional agency aesthetics, I explored a Retro-Vintage Art direction through a collage-based approach. I combined retro color palettes, vintage Egyptian visual references, and collage compositions to create a unique visual mixture.

The result was a fresh and unconventional look that gave the brand a different visual personality and a recognizable creative direction.
